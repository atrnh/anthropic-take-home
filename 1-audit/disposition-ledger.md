## How to read the ledger

Use the task-family sections to review one part of the slice at a time:

Each source link opens the pinned page. The adjacent section names identify the evidence. Current paths are relative to `https://claude.com`; proposed paths are not deployed. **P1** means instructions can be misapplied or a consequential condition can be missed. **P2** means readers must navigate between tasks or reconcile overlapping explanations. **P3** means localized duplication or wording. **Keep** means no structural change is justified by this pass, not that every technical claim has been verified.

**Bridge** means retain the old URL with concise, context-labeled links to its tasks and real targets for its old anchors. It must not keep a second independently authored manual. **Compose** means separately maintain complete applicable procedures and show them at one task destination. Short, identical steps can share a source without collapsing differences in permissions, data paths, packaging, or outcomes.

For every retained page, retain its heading anchors. For every moved section, inventory rendered IDs before publication, retain a useful legacy target, and link to the named task and context. Browser fragments do not reach the server, so section-specific routing needs a retained page or client handling. The `.md` endpoints recorded in the manifest also need a disposition. These rules apply to every row; the tables call out exceptions and context requirements.
