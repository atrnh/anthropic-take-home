To add, change, or remove tools, deploy the change to your server—no resubmission to Anthropic is required, and there is no scheduled re-review. Claude picks up the new tool surface on the next connection.12 13 ## Update your plugin14 15 Plugin updates are pushed via your GitHub repo. CI mirrors changes to the public marketplace and runs automated screening on each update.16 17 ## Update your listing18 19 Edit your description, categories, icon, and other listing metadata from the submissions dashboard at [Organization settings > Directory](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/directory/submissions) in Claude.ai. See [Managing your listing](/docs/connectors/building/managing-your-listing) for what you can edit directly and which changes require review.20 21 ## Slugs are permanent22 23 Your directory slug is fixed after publication. It determines your connector's permanent listing URL:24 25 ```text theme={null}26 https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/SLUG27 ```28 29 Share this URL from your own documentation or a "Connect to Claude" button to send users directly to your listing. Display names can change via the dashboard; the URL slug cannot.30 31 ## Delist your connector32 33 To voluntarily remove your connector from the directory, email `mcp-review@anthropic.com`.
